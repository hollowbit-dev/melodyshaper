First release.

- Hum a tune and hear it on a real instrument, in key and on the beat.
- Get three chord progressions that fit the notes you hummed.
- Beatbox a beat, record your voice, or play the computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard.
- 23 recorded instruments, an 11-piece drum kit, up to 16 tracks, and whole songs with sections and endings.
- Export MP3, WAV, stems or MIDI. Free, offline, no account, and no AI.

Mac: right-click Melodyshaper and choose Open the first time. Windows: if SmartScreen appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

The Windows version is built and its logic is tested, but it hasn't been tried on a real Windows PC yet. If anything goes wrong, please open an issue.
