- 43 recorded instruments, up from 23, including harpsichord, solo violin, harmonica, tuba, bassoon, ocarina and folk harp.
- 29 drum sounds and 119 drum presets across rock, funk, hip-hop, dance, Latin, reggae, jazz, folk and world styles.
- Make a melody from your chords or bass line, in a simple, flowing or catchy style. It uses fixed music rules, and every sound is a real recording.
- Plug in a guitar with a USB cable or audio interface. Choose the input, hear yourself, tune up, record, and change the amp sound afterwards.
- Turn single guitar notes into editable notes.
- Pick a MIDI device, record from keyboards or MIDI guitars, and use a sustain pedal.

Mac: right-click Melodyshaper and choose Open the first time. Windows: if SmartScreen appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

Guitar and MIDI input have only been tested with simulated signals so far, and the Windows version hasn't been tried on a real PC yet. If anything goes wrong, please open an issue.
